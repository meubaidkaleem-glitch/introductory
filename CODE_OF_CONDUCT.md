# Code of Conduct

## Our promise
We want participation to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind and assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome beginners and explain feedback clearly.
- Keep jokes inclusive and appropriate.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, or publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting
Contact the repository owner through the GitHub profile or repository reporting tools. Please include enough context to understand the concern and avoid public disclosure of sensitive details.

Maintainers should respond fairly and proportionately to reports.
